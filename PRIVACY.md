# Detent privacy policy

Detent does not collect, share or sell any data about you.

- **No servers.** Detent has no servers and no accounts. The iPhone, iPad and Mac apps never connect to the internet.
- **Your secrets stay with you.** Two-factor accounts are stored in the keychain on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end; Detent's developer cannot read them.
- **No analytics, tracking or ads.**
- **Camera and photos.** The camera, and any photo or screenshot you choose, are used only on your device to read 2FA setup QR codes. Images are not stored or sent anywhere.
- **AutoFill.** The AutoFill extension reads the site address the system passes to it only to suggest the matching account, and remembers that site with the account.
- **Mac browser access.** The Mac app asks your browser for the address of the page showing a QR code you scan, so it can suggest that account there later. The address stays with the account.
- **Chrome extension.** The extension asks the `detent` tool on your Mac for codes, only on sites you have added. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Backups you export are files you control, encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/colinbm/detent/issues.
